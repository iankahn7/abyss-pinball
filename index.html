      DIVE BRIEFING

      HOW TO PLAY

      1. Hit the 3 SONAR targets to open the tentacles guarding the KRAKEN LOCK.
      2. Lock 3 balls to trigger KRAKEN RELEASED, a 3-ball multiball.
      3. During multiball, the ABYSS JACKPOT (center target) is lit. Each hit pays more.
      4. Make the TEMPEST RAMP and the SONAR ORBIT to light the jackpot on one ball.
      5. Chain major shots within 4 seconds for up to a 5x combo multiplier.
      6. Plunge into the TRENCH LANE (left lane) for a skill shot. The DIVE GATE kickback saves one left-outlane drain.
